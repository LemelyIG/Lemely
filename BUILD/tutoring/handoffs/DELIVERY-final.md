## §5.7 Tutoring marketplace (limitations text; the lead merges into §5)

Drawn from SPEC §i.3, §i.8, §i.9 and §i.10, and from the live and security records. Each bullet is a limitation, stated plainly.

- **Nothing happens on a timer except email delivery.** The 60-second sweeper (§5.3) gets one job: sending queued booking and safeguarding emails, once each. A failed email stays `failed` and is never retried. An email can wait while no instance is running (Cloud Run scales to zero), and a crash at the wrong moment can send one twice. Pending requests whose start passes are shown as expired; nothing changes them in the database until that student next requests that tutor. There are no reminders and no automatic completion.
- **Email may be off without a failure.** Without `[email] api_key` (`RESEND_API_KEY`), tutoring emails are recorded as `skipped` with reason `mock_provider`, startup logs a warning, and the admin queue says "Email delivery: mock". The mock provider was the only one exercised in this build. At most 60 a day are sent, because the free tier allows 100 and is shared with sign-up mail.
- **No payments.** The marketplace shows no prices and takes no payments; fees are agreed outside Lemely (§4).
- **"Documents seen" means a Lemely admin looked at documents the tutor uploaded.** It is not an identity check, a check with the issuer, or a criminal-records check. After a listed tutor changes documents the badge stays cleared until an admin looks again.
- **Tutor documents live in a bucket that an operator must create.** `scripts/gcp-bootstrap.sh` creates `<project>-tutor-evidence-staging` and `-production` (private, uniform access); `deploy.yml` names them. The storage backend refuses the shared default name and checks, before first use, that the bucket is private and belongs to this project. That check has been tested only against fakes; it has not run against a real bucket, and the opt-in live-GCS tests were not run. Uploads are limited to 20 attempts per profile per 24 hours and 200 attempts from all profiles per 60 minutes. The extraction worker is shared with marking, so a burst under those limits can still slow marking. Upload attempt rows are never pruned.
- **Moderation is reactive.** Nothing reads notes, reviews or replies automatically. Review text and replies are read by an admin before they are public; notes are read only when someone reports them. Free text is checked for phone numbers, emails, links and social handles, and meeting links must come from Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Whereby; a determined person can spell a number out in words. A report's details are not run through that check.
- **Child-safety choices made against the architect's recommendation** (ADR-T02): a student of any age can share all their progress with a tutor, without a parent's approval and until they stop it; parents are told and can see who the child shares with and each time a tutor looked, but cannot stop it; notes between a tutor and a minor are free text. A student can remove a parent link at any time and the parent is told once. A student chooses who their linked parents are (up to 4). Notices to parents are not approvals.
- **Completion is recorded by the tutor**, or by the student or the parent who booked if the tutor has recorded nothing 48 hours after the session. Ratings count each student once.
- **Outcome figures inherit the marking accuracy of §5.1.** They rest on automatic marking, leave out grades from estimated boundaries, and students choose their tutors, so they are not evidence of what a tutor caused. Students are counted unless they opt out. A figure appears only when at least 5 students have a marked paper, only when no single student's grade could be worked out from it on its own, and is hidden for 90 days after anyone's counting choice changes.
- **Accepted residuals in the outcome figures** (decision row 13; ADR-T04 R5, R8, R9). R5: a tutor can sometimes work out one student's grade by comparing the figure over time, as students enter 120 days after their first session, five at a time. R8: the 90-day hold stops a reading straight after a counting change but not one taken after the hold ends. R9: grades are not frozen at the close instant, so deleting or restoring an attempt or a regrade can move a published figure outside any hold. No action by the tutor causes these; they only need to look on the right day.
- **"Logged in" is not a strong boundary.** Any free student account with a confirmed email can see full profiles, reviews and outcome figures. Reviews show "A student" and a season, but a tutor with few students can often guess who wrote one, and the rating count changes when a review is added. There is no limit on how long after a session a review can be written (O-16).
- **Search engines can list tutors** (decision row 17, chosen over the recommended "never index"). A listed tutor's public card (first name and initial, headline, subjects, modes, city) can appear in search results unless the tutor turns that off; onboarding says so. The directory page is never indexed. An opt-out reaches the edge within a minute, but a search engine drops a card only when it next visits it, which can take weeks. If the edge check fails for any reason the card is marked not to be indexed. The self-hosted nginx deployment never lets search engines index tutor pages. There is no sitemap. **Staging check not run.**
- **Late cancellation.** A student or parent cannot cancel inside 24 hours of the start. The tutor can, with a reason that the student and linked parents see; notifications and emails never carry the reason.
- **No per-IP rate limit in the application** on the public directory (O-9). Responses are cached at the edge for 60 seconds, which spares the database but does not slow a scraper. A Cloudflare rate-limit rule is an ops step; whether the plan offers one has not been checked. Booking and report routes answer 429 without a `Retry-After` header.
- **Block masking has residuals** (SPEC §i.8, accepted): the public tutor card and a parent's tutor view still show a tutor who blocked the student; a block does not record which side blocked; a student's block switches the tutor's notes to frozen at one instant, which a watching tutor can infer; after mutual blocks, lifting the earlier one moves the recorded block time; a tutor who files their own safety report can tell a freeze from a block by whether the report is accepted.
- **Reports** (SPEC §i.9): one lock per user is held for the report transaction; a safety concern over an open report upgrades it in place. A rejected reply cannot be replaced (one reply per review). Blocks are student and tutor only; parents cannot block (O-18).
- **Access log details**: audited tutor reads retry once on a network drop or a server error, so one visit can be logged twice; a grant's date is shown as a day in the tutor's zone, never the time; admin reads of notes and evidence are logged in `tutoring_admin_access` but not shown in the student's log (O-17).
- **No retention job.** The tutor access log, the admin read log, `parent_link_events`, `email_outbox` and `tutor_evidence_uploads` grow without bound. Accounts that own a tutor profile or are in a booking cannot be deleted until an offboarding procedure exists (O-12). Downgrading past `0049` destroys the access log. A retention period is undecided.
- **Other limits.** Booking emails to legacy placeholder parent addresses are skipped, and SMS stays the mock, so phone-only parents get in-app notices only (O-19). Re-joining a tutor's school-less class after a block or suspension is not refused; the linked parents are told. A Cloud Run revision rollback across this release needs the `DELETE FROM notifications` in `docs/deployment.md` §5.7 first.
- **Frontend evidence.** The tutoring screens were audited on the live stack before the last develop merge (axe clean, Lighthouse accessibility 100, performance 74 to 87). The whole-product audit still fails on 21 violations in files outside the tutoring feature (see §5.5). Touch targets under 44 px remain on a few shared primitives on touch devices (radio and checkbox rows at 41 px, the marketing wordmark link, the parent header icon buttons, the email-verification banner). The screen for a report's detail shows the stored snapshot in raw form (for example `in_person` and an ISO timestamp), and the tutor's student view prints an ISO date in one sentence and uses `->` in the at-risk summary.
- **Browser journeys are not in CI.** The five tutoring journeys and the existing journeys run live only. The last full run passed 62 of 63; the failure is an old string mismatch in `student-journey` that this branch did not cause.

## Operator steps before first production deploy

Copied from `BUILD/tutoring/handoffs/W8.md`.

1. Run `PROJECT_ID=<id> ./scripts/gcp-bootstrap.sh` (again, if already bootstrapped). It creates `gs://<id>-tutor-evidence-{staging,production}` (uniform access, public access prevention), grants the runtime account object access, and grants `lemely-deployer` legacyBucketReader on that bucket only.
2. Confirm the `GCP_WORKLOAD_IDENTITY_PROVIDER` repository variable starts with `projects/<project number>/` (`deploy.yml` reads the number from it).
3. If the bucket create fails with "name not available", do not deploy: another project owns the name.
4. Run `gcloud storage buckets describe gs://<id>-tutor-evidence-<env> --format='value(public_access_prevention,uniform_bucket_level_access)'`. It must show `enforced` and `True`.
5. After the first staging deploy, upload a tutor document and open it from the admin page (A2). The deploy-time owner and privacy check has only been tested against fakes.
6. Decisions still open: retention for the audit, outbox, `parent_link_events` and admin-access tables; the WAF rate-limit threshold and whether the Cloudflare plan has rate-limiting rules; the row 17 staging `curl -sI` indexing checklist (not run).

Also from `docs/deployment.md` §5.9: set `RESEND_API_KEY` (`[email] api_key`) if tutoring emails are wanted; without it they are recorded as skipped.
